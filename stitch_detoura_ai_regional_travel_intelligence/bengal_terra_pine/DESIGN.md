## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, intelligent regional travel ethos. The visual language bridges editorial sophistication with precise, data-dense travel intelligence. 

The aesthetic is grounded in a modern premium travel-tech direction:
- **Atmosphere:** Earthy, tactile, and cultured without being antiquarian. It channels the architectural clay of Bishnupur, the lush canopies of the Dooars, and the tea terraces of Darjeeling into a crisp, high-utility digital workbench.
- **Visual Stance:** Editorial Minimalism paired with geographic precision. Surfaces rely on warm sand and chalk canvas layers rather than synthetic stark whites or harsh dark modes.
- **Emotional Intent:** Discovery, geographic authority, cultural reassurance, and effortless itinerary planning.
- **Style Rules:** No generic high-opacity glassmorphism, rainbow gradients, or loud neons. Visual depth is established through muted tonal layering, crisp borders, and targeted accents of burnt terracotta and deep forest teal.

## Colors

The palette balances warm earthen foundations with rich, bio-regional highlights for AI match ratings and geospatial markers.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - `surface-base`: `#fbfaf7` (Primary background, warm off-white)
  - `surface-subtle`: `#f4f1ea` (Secondary paneling, card wells, structured gutters)
  - `surface-card`: `#ffffff` (Elevated cards and modal surfaces)
  - `surface-border`: `#e8e4dc` (Low-contrast tectonic dividers)
  - `surface-border-strong`: `#d1cbbf` (Interactive form borders, chip outlines)
- **Primary Accent (Terracotta / Bishnupur Clay):**
  - Base: `#c85a32` (Primary interactive targets, key waypoints, high-value actions)
  - Hover/Active: `#af4b27`
  - Subtle Tint: `#fdf2ee` (Terracotta badge backgrounds, highlight pill fills)
- **Secondary Accent (Sundarban Pine & Sub-Himalayan Teal):**
  - Base: `#1b4944` (AI match tags, verified route signals, confidence badges, active route legs)
  - Light/Hover: `#266b64`
  - Subtle Tint: `#edf5f4` (Match score backgrounds, low-intensity metric tiles)
- **Tertiary Accent (Warm Ochre / Mustard):**
  - Base: `#d4973b` (Seasonal warnings, cultural fest indicators, weather advisories)
  - Subtle Tint: `#fbf5eb`
- **Typography & Neutrals:**
  - `text-primary`: `#1c1f24` (Headlines, prominent figures, critical route nodes)
  - `text-secondary`: `#494e57` (Descriptions, editorial body copy, metadata)
  - `text-muted`: `#787f8c` (Timestamps, micro-captions, inactive route states)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is split into three functional roles:
- **Headlines (Epilogue):** Chosen for its crafted, humanist architectural weight. It carries literary dignity suitable for destination narratives, regional heritage names, and route highlights.
- **Body & Editorial (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers clean optical rhythm, warm apertures, and high legibility across long itinerary descriptions, travel advisories, and hotel overviews.
- **Data, Coordinates & Metrics (Space Grotesk):** Drives data clarity for map markers, transit durations, AI match percentages, elevation markers, and pricing indices.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform uses an 8pt base spatial unit combined with an asymmetrical split-pane layout model designed for map-heavy travel intelligence.

### Breakpoints & Layout Model
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column grid or dual-engine view: a 480px fixed itinerary sidebar paired with a fluid geospatial viewport. Maximum container width for pure editorial views is 1320px with 56px (`margin-desktop`) margins and 24px gutters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout. Sidebar transitions to a collapsible slide-over sheet or half-screen bottom card stack. Margins sit at 32px (`margin-tablet`) with 20px gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px margins (`margin-mobile`) and 12px gutters. Dynamic map-to-list toggle or sticky draggable bottom sheet with snapping states (peek: 84px, half: 45vh, expanded: 92vh).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids excessive opacity or diffused modern blurs. Instead, it relies on warm, earth-tinted ambient drop shadows layered over soft border demarcations.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Layer):** Flat `surface-base` (`#fbfaf7`). Zero shadow. Used for base canvas backgrounds.
- **Level 1 (Card Level):** `surface-card` (`#ffffff`) bounded by a subtle `1px solid #e8e4dc` border and an ambient downward cast: `0 2px 8px -1px rgba(36, 39, 44, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(36, 39, 44, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover Cards / Overlays):** Used for hovered itinerary tiles and map preview clusters: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(27, 73, 68, 0.07), 0 3px 6px -2px rgba(36, 39, 44, 0.04)`, border shifted to `surface-border-strong`.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Persistent Map Controllers):** For filter menus, floating search bars, and full route wayfinders: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(28, 31, 36, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(28, 31, 36, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses a calibrated `Rounded` (Level 2) geometry:
- **Default Elements (Inputs, Standard Badges, Buttons):** `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Cards, Panels & Modal Containers:** `1rem` to `1.25rem` (16px to 20px), balancing modern travel ergonomics with structural clarity.
- **Tags, Filter Chips, AI Predictors & Map Controls:** Full pill shape (`9999px`) to maintain strong silhouette distinction against structured square card grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled terracotta (`#c85a32`), hover state `#af4b27`, text `#ffffff` (`Space Grotesk`, medium/semibold). 10px 20px padding, 8px radius. Subtle directional transition.
- **Secondary:** Filled forest pine (`#1b4944`), text `#ffffff`. Used for intelligence exports, booking confirmations, and navigation initiation.
- **Outlined / Tertiary:** Transparent base, `1.5px solid #d1cbbf` border, `text-primary` (`#1c1f24`) typography. Hover yields `#f4f1ea` background.
- **Map Floating Action Buttons:** Circular or rounded-pill, white surface, Level 2 elevation shadow with matching terracotta/teal active icon state.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Filter Chips:** 32px height, full pill roundedness. Default state has a `surface-card` fill with `#e8e4dc` border. Active state features a `#1b4944` background with crisp white text.
- **Match Score Badges (AI Travel Score):** Pill container with `#edf5f4` fill, `#1b4944` text, featuring an integrated numeric lead (e.g., "94% MATCH") set in `Space Grotesk` `label-md`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Search Bar (Itinerary / Destination Engine):** Height 48px to 56px, `surface-card` fill with a subtle 1px border. Deep slate text and muted placeholder. Focus state brings a `2px solid #c85a32` ring without fuzzy outer glow.
- **Form Controls:** Labels set in `Space Grotesk` uppercase tracking (`label-sm`). Inner padding 12px 16px, background `#ffffff`, crisp borders at `#d1cbbf`.

### Cards (Destination & Itinerary Items)
- **Container:** `surface-card` background, 16px–20px corner radius, Level 1 shadow, 1px `#e8e4dc` stroke.
- **Image Integration:** Full-bleed top image aspect ratio 16:10 with an inset terracotta micro-badge for district taxonomy (e.g., "Kalimpong", "Purulia").
- **Content Hierarchy:** Destination name (`headline-sm`), followed by 2-line preview (`body-sm`), separated by a hairline divider leading to transit duration and match score pill.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkbox:** 18px square with 4px corner radius. `#ffffff` surface, `#d1cbbf` border. Checked state fills with `#1b4944` and displays a crisp white check vector.
- **Radio:** 18px circular with matching pine dot when active.

### Specialized Travel Tokens
- **Route Line Markers:** 2px dashed terracotta path indicating heritage trails; solid 3px pine path indicating verified roadways.
- **Elevation / Transit Pill:** Compact Space Grotesk tag (`label-sm`) with soft ochre tint (`#fbf5eb`) displaying altitude (e.g., "2,134 m") or journey time.
